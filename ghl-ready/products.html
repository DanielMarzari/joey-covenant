Medicare, Explained Simply

Every senior's situation is different. Here are the plans and products we help our clients make sense of — in plain English, with no pressure to pick one today.

Core Medicare Plans

The building blocks of your coverage

Original Medicare is split into four parts, and most people pair it with a supplement or choose an all-in-one Advantage plan. We'll walk you through what fits.

A
Original Medicare

Medicare Part A

Hospital coverage. Pays for inpatient care when you're admitted. Most people pay no monthly premium thanks to their (or a spouse's) work history.

  • Inpatient hospital stays
  • Skilled nursing facility care
  • Hospice and end-of-life care
  • Limited home health services
Ask us about Medicare Part A →
B
Original Medicare

Medicare Part B

Doctor and outpatient coverage. Pays for the care you get outside the hospital. Comes with a monthly premium and annual deductible set by Medicare.

  • Doctor visits and specialist care
  • Preventive screenings and vaccines
  • Outpatient procedures and labs
  • Durable medical equipment
Ask us about Medicare Part B →
C
Medicare Advantage

Medicare Part C

The all-in-one alternative to Original Medicare, sold by private insurers. Bundles Parts A and B (usually D too), often with extras like dental, vision, and a fitness benefit.

  • Combines Parts A, B, and often D
  • Extras: dental, vision, hearing, gym
  • Network-based (HMO or PPO)
  • Annual out-of-pocket maximum
Compare Advantage plans →
D
Prescription Drugs

Medicare Part D

Prescription drug coverage. Sold as a standalone plan alongside Original Medicare, or built into most Medicare Advantage plans.

  • Tiered pricing on common medications
  • Pairs with Original Medicare or bundled in Advantage
  • Annual formulary review recommended
  • Helps avoid the late-enrollment penalty
Review Your Prescription Coverage →
S
Medigap

Medicare Supplement

Pays the out-of-pocket costs Original Medicare leaves behind — deductibles, coinsurance, and copays. Pairs with Parts A & B.

  • Covers most Medicare cost-sharing
  • Use any doctor that accepts Medicare
  • No network restrictions
  • Predictable monthly costs
Find the right Medigap plan →
Added Protection

Fill the gaps Medicare leaves behind

Medicare doesn't cover everything. These optional products help protect your savings from the costs Original Medicare and Advantage plans don't fully cover.

DVH
Everyday Care

Dental, Vision & Hearing

Original Medicare doesn't cover routine dental, vision, or hearing care. A DVH policy fills those gaps with predictable copays and allowances.

  • Cleanings, fillings, and major dental work
  • Eye exams, glasses, and contacts
  • Hearing exams and hearing aid allowances
  • Use with any Medicare plan
Add DVH coverage →
$
Cash Benefit

Hospital Indemnity

Pays you cash directly for every day you're admitted to the hospital. Use it for deductibles, groceries, travel for a family member — whatever you need.

  • Fixed daily cash benefit
  • Pays on top of Medicare
  • Spend the money however you want
  • Great pair with a Medicare Advantage plan
Protect against hospital stays →
+
Critical Illness

Cancer, Heart Attack & Stroke

A lump-sum cash payout when you're diagnosed with cancer, a heart attack, or a stroke. Helps cover treatment costs, travel, or time away from work.

  • Lump-sum payment at diagnosis
  • Covers the most common major illnesses
  • Use the money for any expense
  • Works alongside any Medicare plan
Learn about critical-illness →
FE
Whole Life

Final Expense Insurance

A small whole-life policy designed to cover funeral, burial, and end-of-life costs, so your family isn't left with the bill.

  • Typically $10,000 to $30,000 in coverage
  • Locked-in premium for life
  • Usually no medical exam required
  • Peace of mind for loved ones
Plan for final expenses →
RC
Extended Care

Recovery Care

Covers extended recovery at home, in assisted living, or in a nursing facility — including the costs Medicare stops paying for after Day 100. Protects your savings if you ever need ongoing care.

  • Skilled nursing after Medicare's Day 100 cutoff
  • In-home aides and nursing care
  • Assisted living and custodial care
  • Shields savings from catastrophic costs
Explore Recovery Care →
