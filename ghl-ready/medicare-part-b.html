Original Medicare

Medicare Part B

Part B is the medical half of Original Medicare. It pays 80% of approved costs — you pay 20%. The catch? That 20% has no cap.

What Medicare Part B Covers

Pretty much everything outside the hospital walls.

✓

Doctor & Specialist Visits

Office visits, second opinions, and most consultations with providers who accept Medicare.

✓

Outpatient Procedures

Same-day surgeries and procedures performed in clinics or outpatient hospital settings.

✓

Lab Work, X-Rays & Imaging

Blood tests, X-rays, MRIs, CT scans, and other diagnostic imaging.

✓

Chemo, Radiation & Injectables

Most cancer treatments delivered on an outpatient basis, plus injectable medications.

✓

Durable Medical Equipment

Wheelchairs, walkers, oxygen, CPAP machines, and other doctor-prescribed equipment.

✓

Ambulance & Emergency Room

Medically necessary ambulance transport and emergency room visits.

Original Medicare’s greatest flaw: the 20% has no CAP — ever.

Medicare pays 80% of approved costs. You pay 20%. On a $100,000 cancer treatment, that's $20,000 out of pocket. On a $500,000 treatment, $100,000. There's no annual stop-loss limit. This is why most seniors pair Part B with a Medicare Supplement or choose Medicare Advantage.
